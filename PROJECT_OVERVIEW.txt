  iCook: Introduction & Overview
  “Your Kitchen, Your Creativity.”
================================================================================


INTRODUCTION
------------

iCook is a social cooking app built for home cooks.

Cooking at home is rarely just about following a recipe. It’s also about
inspiration, the ingredients you already have, and the people you cook
alongside: friends, family, or a wider community. iCook brings those
pieces together in one place.

The app is designed as a everyday kitchen companion: discover recipes,
cook smarter with your pantry, share what you make, and stay connected
with other cooks. The focus is creativity in your own kitchen, not
restaurant cooking or professional meal prep.


THE IDEA
--------

Most cooking apps lean hard in one direction: either a recipe library,
a meal planner, or a social feed. iCook’s concept is to combine the
practical and the social:

  • Practical: find recipes, track what’s in your pantry, and match
    dishes to ingredients you already own.
  • Creative: save favorites, create your own recipes, and post what
    you’re cooking.
  • Social: follow other cooks, explore a community feed, and message
    people who share your taste in food.

The product vision is simple: help people cook more often, with more
confidence, using what they have, and feel part of a cooking community
while they do it.


OVERVIEW
--------

iCook is organized around a few core experiences:

  Discover
    Browse recipes and cooking posts from the community. Like, comment,
    and share content that inspires you.

  Recipes
    Explore recipes by category, open detailed guides with ingredients
    and steps, and save the ones you want to cook later.

  Pantry
    Keep track of ingredients at home. Search and suggestions can favor
    recipes that fit what you already have, so less food goes to waste
    and planning feels easier.

  Create
    Publish your own recipes or short cooking posts. Your kitchen
    becomes part of the feed, not only a place you consume content.

  Profile & community
    Build a cook profile, follow others, see activity, and browse saved
    or created recipes. Messaging lets you stay in touch with cooks you
    connect with.

  Personalization
    Themes, accents, and settings let the app feel like yours; security
    options support a proper account experience.


WHO IT’S FOR
------------

iCook is aimed at everyday home cooks: people who want ideas for dinner,
help using what’s in the fridge, a place to save what works, and a light
social layer around cooking. It is not aimed at professional chefs or
commercial kitchens.


IN ONE LINE
-----------

iCook is a social kitchen app that connects recipe discovery, pantry-smart
cooking, and a community of home cooks.
